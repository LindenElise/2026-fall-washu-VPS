My Website is...

People don’t use personalized websites, because it is mostly big companies now who have made sharing information easier. However, this causes a loss in personality because social media is so broad and streamlined. Besides sharing pictures or videos, there is not much else you can do. Learning HTML allows you to really personalize the website you make, and have more interaction and engagement with your site. Making just an archive creates a dead website. So interaction or something interesting or unique can help Having smaller confined space and regulations is good in a world of overload of info, so having a space just for your work can allow people to engage and be more focused on just your creations without competition from other users (like on social media).
