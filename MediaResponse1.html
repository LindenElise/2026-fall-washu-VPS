Media Response 1: Eno

Feeling is where a thought starts Art blooming into something- planting it then seeing to grow Want to belong, be part of group, share thoughts, Synchronizing, singing… Starting point is important to how the product turns out Humans becoming specialized Open ended - allows viewer to feel and imagine Airport - getting in a headspace where we don’t feel as important - we are just one person in a world of 8bil - insignificance to make death not as scary Humans have a need to belong and feel accepted, and music and art has that ability to connect to a larger people. He mentioned how when were are in a concert, we are not one person, but like a whole being, connected by the music, and the love for that music or musician. Art and music carries on this idea through the message and feeling of it. He also mentioned how a lot of times, we have quite similar feelings, to others, but artists make that feeling into a “physical” adaptation to interpretation of that feeling. Then those who feel really closely to that feeling or idea may resonate with that piece of artwork or music. Another common theme in this documentary, he talked about how his art grows, and how he lets his art grow. He doesn’t call his work “done” because it evolves into something new. He uses randomization and his feelings to generate new combinations of music, but it still holds its core from the beginning. I also liked the idea he mentioned when talking about ambience and ambient music. It allows ideas to grow, and allows us to imagine. When we are given space and time to be alone with our thoughts, we are able to evolve them into new things. He also mentioned this idea in relation to his breakfast—when he stopped his constant intake of info in the morning, it allowed him to think for himself — to fill in the silence with his own imagination (similar to the idea if ambiance)
