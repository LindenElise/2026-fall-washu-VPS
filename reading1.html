Reading Response 1: Restaurant Websites

This reading emphasized the importance of creating and designing websites for restaurants because of the websites ability to showcase their menu online as well as give the viewer an idea of the atmosphere at the restaurant. The website does not have to be super complex or well made, but it will reflect the restaurant itself. One beneift of creating a website from scratch with html or css is that it is more customizable and allows personality. Templates from Squarespace or other webstie making places do not allows the level of customization and can detract from the websites personality.
