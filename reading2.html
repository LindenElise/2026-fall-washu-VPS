Reading Response 2: The New Turing Test

The New Turing Test

When art is made from someone, for someone, and has a certain context, there is no aspect or process of it that can be replaced by AI without the product feeling tainted by generic responses or insincere ideas. AI takes away the process of art and produces an immediate product. Yet, the process is what makes the art meaningful, along with its original human made idea. AI combines every artwork, every idea, every piece of writing etc. that was given to it, and when asked, produces a unthoughtful combination of everything, in order to complete its task. More recently, AI is quite capable of making realistic or “good” looking art, yet that is probably the least interesting aspect of art. The core of art comes from the "emotional resonance" which cannot be replicated by a machine. Only a human could make “bad” looking art that is truly profound in its meaning. But AI gaining the ability to replicate human work should not discourage, but rather inspire to prove that we cannot be replaced.
