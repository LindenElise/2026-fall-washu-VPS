Reading Response 4: The Forgotten Zone

The Forgotten Zone

I love this idea of containers and organizing. I am someone who loves to organize and make sure I know where things are. I regularly clean out and re-organize pinterest boards so that they represent who I am at the time. In real life, I have plenty of boxes that I organize, getting rid of things I don’t need anymore.

I liked how in the article, they talked about the container being a larger context for what is within. I feel like this is like personality and expression, where a person is the container, and their thoughts, feelings and interests are the things being contained. Allowing expression on the outside, allows context and information to connect for the info within.

I think having a place to go back to if you have an art block, or maybe don't have many ideas, is a great resource to have. One aspect I liked about one of the websites described in the article was the button that put two different works side by side, giving each work a new context. This could allow so many unique ideas, and might help art block, or continue a creative flow.
