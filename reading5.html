Reading 5: The Coming Age of Calm Technology

As the internet evolves, there is one main factor that shapes its development, and that is in pursuit of people's attention. People tend to cerate relationships with technology, and grow attatched to it, such as their phone. This connection is contributing to the rise of UC and AI - the "smart" item era. Everything has memmory in a way and can change based on past events or things you have seen. This is moving away from the more indivisual pieces of technology that complete a simple task, such as a toster, or a microwave, and transitioning into more interactive, helpful, or "calm," technology. Calm technology aims to gain both our peripheral and centeral attention.
