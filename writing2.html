Singing is a human-made creation/practice that beings out the best in me. As a kid, my and my sibling took singin lessons, then stopped. Then throughout my life, I would just sing for fun - usually when no one was in the house. This would happen so much so that I would record myself and listen back to improve.

Singing as a more societal process is also important. Singing has been used as a form of communication - of stories and emotions. I have noticed how impactful singing has been for me when I do it myself, and when I hear it - especially when there is a love singer. There is something so impactful about singing, even if there are no lyrics, or the words are not in a language I speak, it allows feelings and emotions to be convyed in such a uniquly human way.
